## 2026-09-25 - Interactive Feedback on Card Hover/Focus States
**Learning:** For static portfolio and card-based web apps, subtle `border-color` and `transform: translateY(-2px)` transitions on `:hover` and `:focus-within` provide immediate affordance without disrupting layout or keyboard navigation accessibility.
**Action:** Apply `:hover` and `:focus-within` visual elevation transitions on interactive grid cards in pure HTML/CSS sites.
